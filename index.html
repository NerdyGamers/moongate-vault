About the Vault

The Moongate Vault is a community library of Ultima Online client assets. Files are hosted by their artists — on GitHub, Google Drive, Terabox, or wherever they keep them — and the Vault links out to them. Nothing here is re-hosted or claimed by the Vault itself.

Artists: every work is credited to its original artist by name. If a piece is yours and the credit is wrong, or you want it removed, tell the Keeper.

To offer a work for the Vault, or to open your own page of works, visit the Vault itself and create an account — submissions are reviewed by the Keeper before they go live.
